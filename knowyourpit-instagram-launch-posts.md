## Posting notes

- **Order:** Start with Post 1 (launch), then alternate between feature posts. Don't run two "here's what it does" posts back-to-back — break them up with real cook content if you have it.
- **Reels opportunity:** Posts 2 and 4 work well as short Reels (15–30 sec) with a screen recording of the feature in use, no narration needed — let the on-screen text carry it.
- **Story versions:** Crop each caption to the first two lines for Stories. Always end with the tagline and a swipe-up / link-in-bio CTA.
- **Brand name:** Always **knowyourpit** — lowercase, no spaces, never "Know Your Pit."
