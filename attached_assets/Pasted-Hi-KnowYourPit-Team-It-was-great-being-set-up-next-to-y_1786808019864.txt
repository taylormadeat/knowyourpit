Hi KnowYourPit Team,

It was great being set up next to you and competing against you at the Noblesville BrewBQ. After seeing what you guys are building and getting a better feel for KnowYourPit, I kept thinking there could be a really natural partnership between our two brands.

KnowYourPit helps people manage and improve the cook, while Big Pete’s Seasoning helps make that cook taste great. I think there’s an opportunity to put those two things together in a way that benefits both sides.

A partnership could include:

Big Pete’s featured cook plans or recipes inside KnowYourPit, built around different meats and our seasoning blends.
A unique KnowYourPit discount code for Big Pete’s products, giving your users a special offer while allowing us to track sales generated through the partnership.
Affiliate or revenue share opportunities on purchases driven through KnowYourPit.
Product giveaways or BBQ challenges that we can both promote through social media and email.
Cross-promotion, with Big Pete’s introducing our audience to KnowYourPit and KnowYourPit introducing its users to Big Pete’s.
Eventually, recommended Big Pete’s seasonings within certain cook plans or meat profiles if the partnership performs well.
I think the best way to start would be with a 60–90 day pilot so both of us can see what works without making it overly complicated. Big Pete’s could provide seasoning products, recipes and content, an exclusive discount, and promotional support. KnowYourPit could provide agreed-upon in-app, social, and/or email exposure.

Then we could look at things like sales from the discount code, engagement, new customers and users, and overall content performance to see if it makes sense to grow the partnership.

Since we were literally cooking right next to each other at BrewBQ, this feels like a pretty natural way to turn a friendly competition into something that could help both brands grow.

I’d love to hear what you guys think and what a partnership could look like from your side.
